Quality before hype

What counts as “better”?

Same product

Match SKU, EAN, model, quantity and specification. Cheapest is meaningless if the products differ.

Food quality

Use ingredients, origin, organic/PDO/PGI evidence, harvest/processing information and compliant EU labels — not marketing adjectives.

Full basket cost

Product + Ireland delivery + customs + VAT + courier and FX costs. A low foreign shelf price alone is not a deal.

Fresh evidence

Publishable comparisons carry a check date. Old evidence automatically drops out of verified status.
