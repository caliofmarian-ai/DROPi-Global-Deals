Quality before hype

What counts as “better”?

Same product

Match SKU, EAN, model, quantity and specification. Cheapest is meaningless if the products differ.

Food quality

Use ingredients, origin, organic/PDO/PGI evidence, harvest/processing information and compliant EU labels — not marketing adjectives.

Full cost

Product + Ireland delivery + customs + VAT + courier fees. A low foreign shelf price alone is not a deal.

Fresh evidence

Every publishable deal needs dated source evidence. Expired prices are demoted until checked again.
